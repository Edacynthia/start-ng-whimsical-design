Got a game? Put it on start.ng

  1. 1

    Build it

    Any static browser game: HTML, JavaScript, three.js, Phaser. Players bring their start.ng character, scores and friends.

  2. 2

    Push it to GitHub

    A public repo with a ready-to-play index.html at the top or in dist/, plus a cover.png for its card.

  3. 3

    Publish it

    One API call with your repo link, no key needed. Or hand your AI start.ng/docs and let it do the lot.

  4. 4

    Approve it once

    Open the link you get back and it's live at yourgame.start.ng, with its own leaderboard if you want one. After that, every push updates it.
